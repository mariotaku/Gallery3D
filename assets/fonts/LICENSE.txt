These are the Latin subsets served by Google Fonts, which is why they are 44KB
rather than 300KB. Enough for the chrome and for the museum's titles; a script
outside Latin will come out blank rather than as the wrong glyph.

Shipped because the app had no font of its own. It fell back to whatever the
platform had - Segoe UI on Windows, DejaVu on Linux - so the wall looked
different on every machine, and in a browser there is no system font to fall
back to at all, which left every label empty.
